## 1.3.0 - 26/09/2026
- The audit log now logs more details.
- Administrators can sign everyone out from the Users page.
- Administrators can remove Orphan files.

- Support for multi companies and logos with user permissions to view.

## 1.2.1 - 25/09/2026
- The site now works on phones and tablets: the menu folds away behind a menu button, and pages fit the screen.

- Fixed audit log which now records IP addresses correctly.

- New public records page. Add '/public' to end of website.
- Back to top button added.

## 1.2.0 - 24/09/2026
- New COSHH Inventory page: the register of hazardous substances, filled in from the COSHH assessments, with its own PDF.
- "Groups exposed" on each risk assessment hazard is now a set of buttons to tick, instead of free text.
- Administrators have a new Backups page to make a backup straight away, download one, or restore from one.
- The site's data is now backed up automatically once a day, and the last 14 backups are kept.
- Settings, Users, Audit log and Backups are now grouped under one Admin menu.

## 1.1.1 - 23/09/2026
- The Home page shows which assessments are due for review in the next 30 days, and any that are overdue.
- If two people edit the same record, the second save no longer overwrites the first. You're asked what to do instead.
- Administrators have a new Audit log page to search sign-ins and changes, and export them for Excel.
- Buttons now have icons, and the "New assessment" button is easier to find.
- Vendor files now shipped with website files.

## 1.1.0 - 23/09/2026
